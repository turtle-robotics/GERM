# Retention decision proposal — awaiting human approval

2026-11-16. **PROPOSAL ONLY. No deletion is authorized or implemented.** Requirements authority remains `M0-provisional-2026-11-15`, the supplied official PO/SR matrix snapshot; a signed controlled release is unresolved. [M0 source baseline](M0_SOURCE_BASELINE.json) explicitly leaves the storage-retention conflict open. This proposal changes neither that requirement snapshot nor a Gantt status.

## Conflict and decision required

[SR-DAT-02, source sheet1 C16:D16](REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.md) requires at least 90 days of five-minute environmental/operating records, at least one image per hour, a warning at 80% capacity, and automatic removal of oldest images while preserving at least the most recent 30 days. The earlier development plan's approximately 60-second persistence cadence is finer than the five-minute obligation; it is not authority to discard intermediate records. The master assignment prohibits historical deletion without explicit authorization. Subsequent accepted milestones and the current M7 authorization prohibit automatic image, event, export, backup and historical-record deletion. Those current restrictions govern this implementation; implementing the matrix's automatic deletion clause now would violate them.

The image horizon also needs clarification: does the 90-day clause apply to hourly images as well as environmental/operating records, or is 30 days the image floor? Retention of events, maintenance, model/provenance, exports, backup attempts and orphan/partial files has no approved destructive cleanup rule. Deleting image rows/files could break experiment/inference/audit provenance; a verified export is not automatically authorization to delete its source. A backup on the same host volume does not establish protection from drive failure.

## Proposed decision for review

1. Adopt the current **retain-all, warn-and-refuse** behavior as an explicit host-only exception to the automatic-deletion clause. Keep all historical rows, saved images, exports, backups, interrupted stages and recovery reports. The 80% observed hosting-volume warning is informational and labelled as including other applications; no actuator permission changes follow from it.
2. Preserve at least the 90-day/five-minute record obligation and hourly image provenance as unresolved target-capacity acceptance tests, not claims already proved by synthetic tests. Size storage for the actual approved cadence, image dimensions/size, experiment duration and retained artifacts. Propose a 90-day image planning target until the ambiguity is resolved, with no claim that this target is an approved requirement or achievable on the unmeasured target.
3. When capacity/quota is exhausted, report exact refusal and incomplete state. Offer reviewed backup verification and a separately approved storage expansion/off-device-copy procedure. Do not automatically prune, compact away historical rows, overwrite backups, or infer successful remote/off-device delivery.
4. Before any future cleanup code, approve a controlled requirement change or explicitly authorize a precisely scoped policy: data classes, minimum horizons, meaning of volume versus application quota, warning/critical thresholds, legal/project holds, protected active experiments, raw versus aggregated readings, backup count/location, incomplete/orphan artifacts, referential/provenance preservation, audit evidence and recovery procedure. Require separate destructive-action authorization and acceptance tests. Accepting this proposal alone does not enable deletion in software.

The human decision is whether to approve this host-only no-deletion exception and clarify image/event/artifact horizons, or retain SR-DAT-02's future pruning obligation while explicitly deferring its implementation. Neither choice silently grants production cleanup or deployment permission. Until then, M7 has no delete/retention endpoint, worker, configuration flag or scheduled cleanup action.
