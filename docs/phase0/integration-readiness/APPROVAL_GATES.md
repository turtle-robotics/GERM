# Minimum approvals and hold points

**Proposed gates; none executed or granted by this report.** Roles are proposed disciplines, not named/assigned people. The project reviewer must appoint accountable people and independent reviewers, record exact release/configuration/data versions, scope, date, evidence, limits and abort authority. Host acceptance and green CI do not satisfy a physical or production gate. The [40-SR checklist](PHYSICAL_INTEGRATION_CHECKLIST.md) remains provisional.

## Minimum before connecting software to real hardware

G2 is the connection hold point, including its applicable G1 installation/access/data controls and independently isolated hazardous loads. It requires **named electrical/safety, sensor/metrology, software safety, security/deployment/data and validation supervision signoffs**, plus relevant controlled requirements/deviations and explicit project permission for the exact connection. A merge is a separate G0 decision; approval to connect does not silently approve merging or production exposure. Model/crop approval is additionally required before those features operate, rather than being invented as a prerequisite to every passive sensor test.

The accepted runtime's physical guards and missing production boundary are implementation blockers, not merely missing signatures. Authorize/implement/review the physical boundary in a separate software change first; retain M1 as the single command permission/dispatch boundary, forbid configuration/HTTP authority injection and custom uploads, and prove DB/logging/input/restart faults cannot grant permission.

Every gate expires on relevant SHA/wiring/firmware/model/schema/configuration or safety-procedure change until reviewed. Missing evidence, unknown/stale inputs, unexpected output, IO failure, unsafe electrical state or unapproved scope means stop/isolate under the approved procedure, retain records and obtain explicit recovery authorization. No gate authorizes cleanup of history or auto-deletion.
