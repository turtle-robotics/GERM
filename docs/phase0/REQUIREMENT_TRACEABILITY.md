# Requirement and evidence traceability

Source: supplied GERM CDR PO-SR Traceability Matrix.xlsx, sheet1 (OOXML sheet1.xml), coordinates below. Exact wording is retained for software, environment and safety dependencies; status in the source workbook is not changed. SOURCE_BASELINE.json records the upload hash.

Phase 0 tests establish interface behavior only. No requirement is marked accepted by fixture execution. SO-2/3 map to SCHEMA_CONTRACT and SQLite fixture checks; SO-4 maps to API/sensor/offline checks; SO-5 maps to capture/hash/restart fixtures and target recovery procedure. P0-01/02/05 cover state/safety/environment; P0-03/04/07 cover data/offline/UI; P0-08/09 cover camera/ML; P0-06 covers deployment/recovery. Physical electrical/mechanical safety metrics require separate measured evidence.

The development plan's approximately 60 s persistence cadence may be finer than SR-DAT-02's five-minute retention obligation. Retain at least the required five-minute records for 90 days, with <1% missing scheduled records in the 72 h campaign. Its generic warning/critical storage policy is specialized by the matrix: 80% warning/pruning and at least 30 days of images. Missing policies cannot be guessed into a successful test.
