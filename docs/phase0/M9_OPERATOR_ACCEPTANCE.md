# Deferred operator acceptance checklist

**NOT EXECUTED.** This checklist is for separately authorized Raspberry Pi/physical acceptance after engineering and safety review. Host tests do not authorize installation, flashing, energizing, crop settings, model use or production exposure. Record named reviewer, approved procedure/version, date, instrument calibration, raw evidence and pass/fail/block decision for every item. Unchecked items remain open. Map results to [all 40 SRs](M9_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.json); do not update Gantt or requirements statuses without approval.

Before any physical work:

- [ ] Approve a controlled PO/SR release or document each accepted deviation; retain the provisional matrix and its approval history. Resolve SR-DAT-02 retention/image horizons and artifacts explicitly; no deletion is currently authorized.
- [ ] Identify the full reviewed software/firmware stack and installation/rollback procedure, independently verify package hashes and offline dependencies, preserve the original 15 file hashes, untracked work and historical backups. Approve which reviewed commits may merge; no automatic merge.
- [ ] Approve production authentication, read/download authorization, roles, CSRF/Origin/Host policy, network binding/firewall/TLS and OS/service permissions. Simulation authorizers and fixtures must not become production controls. Review quotas and off-device backup/restore; restore remains isolated until separate adoption approval.
- [ ] Electrical reviewer resolves the conflicting source pin assignments against the user's wiring diagram and as-built continuity checks. Record each load, pin, polarity, PWM/timer mapping, voltage/current, safe electrical state and independent interlock/cutoff; no assumed pin defaults.
- [ ] With the approved lockout/isolation procedure, verify wet-zone/DC/mains separation, GFCI, rated wiring/connectors, branch protection and component pressure/material ratings. Capture manufacturer evidence for SR-SAF-04/05/06/08/09 and SR-LIF-01/03; software cannot certify these.

Under a separately approved supervised test procedure:

- [ ] Verify safe startup, shutdown, power interruption and repeated restart. Measure startup under the two-minute SR-AUT-02 target; no old confirmation, E-stop reset, restored DB or recovered sensor may start a load. Retain profile/data and require a separate confirmed Start with approved current inputs.
- [ ] Verify latching physical E-stop removes high-power energy within SR-SAF-01's one second; reset alone must not restart. Independently verify panel cutoff within 0.5 seconds, leak cutoff within two seconds, thermal cutoff/reset thresholds and fuse behavior using the approved electrical procedure. Measure physical outcomes separately from accepted software commands and firmware ACKs. Stop on any discrepancy.
- [ ] Approve each real sensor interface/location/polarity/update period/fault threshold. Calibrate against traceable references across stated temperature/RH ranges. Verify disconnect, invalid/replayed/stale/implausible input detection within two expected periods, clear HMI naming/logging and approved conservative load response. Electronics and canopy temperatures are distinct channels.
- [ ] Verify no leak/wet-electronics exposure and required containment/pressure/filter/mist distribution/recovery/reservoir reserve using the controlled mechanical procedures and instruments. Record six-position dimensional envelope/clearances and the PPFD map; do not infer them from schedules.
- [ ] Approve basil/spearmint settings with horticultural owner. Check configured units, schedules, restart/catch-up policy, missing inputs, maintenance entry/confirmed exit, safe manual tests and safe STOP. Verify actual pump/light/fan states against requests and ACKs, including disconnection/faults.
- [ ] Approve physical camera activation/calibration/mounting/lighting/FOV. Compare saved dimensions/bytes/hash/UTC timestamps/experiment linkage to actual captures; test unavailable/stale images and interruption recovery. Never substitute a placeholder for a real image.
- [ ] Approve local model/manifest/hash/licensing, reviewed label policy and held-out GERM-like dataset with no leakage. Measure SR-ML-01 size/latency and SR-ML-02 balanced accuracy/recall/F1 against specified sample counts. Verify confidence below 0.70 displays uncertainty and reviewed abnormal alerts require two consecutive results. No host fixture is plant-health validation.
- [ ] Test all seven screens on the actual 739×447 touchscreen and keyboard. Measure new-data refresh against two seconds; verify focus, touch targets, scroll/recovery/error states, confirmation cancellation and transport loss. Execute SR-UI-01 with five novice users, orientation/task time/error counts and at least four successful users; preserve raw observations.
- [ ] Verify maintenance access, tool/service times and cleaning intervals against SR-MNT-01–04, and service-life/material evidence against SR-LIF-01–03. A 72-hour host run does not satisfy the 90-day no-replacement requirement or 60-day biological grow demonstration.

Supervised endurance acceptance, only after the above gates:

- [ ] Approve a written supervision/stop/escalation plan before a 72-hour physical run. Use approved crop schedules, calibrated sensors, containment and independent protections. Do not interpret an eventual unattended-operation requirement as permission to run an unreviewed prototype unattended.
- [ ] Record 72 **real elapsed** continuous hours, clock source/monotonic timing, warnings/faults, data cadence/missing fraction (SR-DAT-03 below 1%), all pump/light occurrences, physical outcomes, temperatures, water reserve/refill/reset/replacement events, disk space and operator interventions. State every interruption; do not splice restarts into an uninterrupted pass.
- [ ] Verify offline operation during internet loss and approved remote-alarm delivery/unsent behavior separately. Test targeted faults only under the approved procedure; do not turn a destructive fault campaign into an unqualified continuous-run pass.
- [ ] Verify CSV/archive and backup hashes, durable event/ACK provenance, consistent snapshots during writes, and isolated restore integrity. Keep originals and incomplete attempts. No automatic retention/deletion is permitted until the specific policy is approved.
- [ ] Engineering, safety, requirements and product owners review raw evidence/deviations and sign scope-specific acceptance. Separate software host readiness, electrical safety, physical endurance, biological outcomes, security and production deployment decisions. Until then: physical and production readiness **not established**.
